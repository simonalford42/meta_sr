# Audit of 373692 versus 709715

Read-only inspection, September 20, 2026. No evaluations or SLURM jobs submitted.

## What evolved

The generation-20 bundle in `runs/373692/best_bundles/best_gen20.jl` combines semantic_backprop_beam_retarget_slim_gen6_3, protected_pareto_clone_niche_survival_gen20_7, simple_parsimony_tournament_gen13_0, and adaptive_winsorized_mae_loss_simple_gen18_2.

The mutation chooses a subtree, numerically inverts the surrounding expression to infer desired subtree outputs from training y, searches small expressions against those targets, fits affine rescalings, and accepts a replacement after checking actual weighted training MSE. It uses a five-offset scan, up to six secant iterations, two beam rounds with width three and a 150-candidate budget, then full-data verification. The synthetic extra feature is a temporary probe in a local copy of X, replaced by an ordinary expression or the original subtree before normal return. No dataset-name lookup, hardcoded benchmark answers, filesystem access, score-field modification, or dataset mutation was found in the four operators. This is static inspection, not an execution-level proof.

Survival protects useful loss/complexity tradeoffs and removes clones; selection is an adaptive-parsimony tournament; loss is absolute error capped at three times its mean. The evolved loss does not directly assign GT fitness: `parallel_eval_pysr.py` invokes `domain.check_solved` on the returned equations separately.

The trajectory supports the mutation as an important contributor, not a controlled causal attribution: fresh-train score rises from 52% at generation zero to 63.5% with semantic retargeting at generation one, 75% with changed selection at generation three, and 83.5% with beam mutation / changed survival / MAE at generation five. This single trial cannot establish that n-runs=3 caused the advantage.

## Compute caveat

`SymbolicRegression.jl/src/Mutate.jl` custom mutation dispatch returns `MutationResult(; tree=tree)`, whose num_evals defaults to zero. Internal `eval_tree_array` calls and beam candidate scoring therefore do not enter that mutation's reported evaluation count. The usual subsequent candidate scoring is counted, but the internal search is not. This is an evaluator-accounting limitation for expensive data-aware mutations, not evidence of malicious intent. Both runs have nominal max_evals=1,000,000, a 500-second PySR timeout, and 600-second outer wall limit. These caps do not establish equal actual compute.

Across the 200 stored result JSONs in each final-eval split, mean runtime_seconds was 367.2 seconds train and 404.5 seconds validation for 373692, versus 125.8 and 138.0 for 709715. These are total recorded task runtimes, not a controlled search-only timing benchmark; versions, compilation and hardware can differ. Neither set contained an error or timed_out flag. Actual num_evaluations is null in these outputs.

## Existing final evaluations

Both summaries use ten runs, seed 192, the same two 20-task splits, max_samples=1000, and the above search limits. Per-task task specifications confirm data_seed=192. They were executed at different dates, so this is an existing-record comparison, not a new controlled rerun. Values below were checked by averaging result_details[].avg_gt; the split-level field named avg_r2 holds the selected fitness here and must not be interpreted as ordinary R-squared.

| Bundle selected for final evaluation | Train GT | Validation GT |
|---|---:|---:|
| 373692 generation 20 | 84.5% | 61.5% |
| 709715 generation 43 (best validation bundle) | 76.0% | 68.5% |

Thus the new run improves training recovery by 8.5 percentage points but loses 7 points on validation. The earlier 90.5% / 65.5% numbers were background fresh-seed evaluations during evolution, not these final evaluations. The final validation difference comes entirely from feynman_I_8_14 (30% versus 100%), feynman_II_11_28 (0% versus 60%), and feynman_II_13_34 (0% versus 10%); all other validation task averages are equal.

709715's final-evaluated bundle uses structural motif duplication with rational templates, age/cost survival, niche/clone-aware selection, and affine-profile loss. Its mutation does not inspect dataset y, unlike the new semantic-retargeting mutation. Its evolution used 45 generations and population reevaluation 3→10, compared with 20 generations and no population reevaluation for 373692.

## Artifact mismatch

The identification pass in 373692's run.log (around lines 5566–5582) rescores only ten initialization bundles and replaces the strong generation-20 winner with symmetric_motif_graft_init_1. Consequently best_final.jl is NOT the strong bundle discussed here. The subsequent validation-based loader explicitly selects the strong generation-20 bundle, and both final_eval_summary.json and the final-eval tasks confirm it was evaluated. Use best_gen20.jl to reference the strong bundle. The identification/archive path warrants a separate fix; no project behavior was modified in this audit.
