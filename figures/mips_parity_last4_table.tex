Expressions are algebraically regrouped and constants rounded to six significant digits; the displayed forms are verified on the complete recorded training and held-out relations within absolute tolerance $10^{-9}$. Recovery pools component successes across seeds; the MIPS-evolved method also recovers all four components in run index 8. Each fit uses at most $10^6$ evaluations and a 500-second timeout.
