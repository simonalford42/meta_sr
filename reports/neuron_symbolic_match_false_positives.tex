\colorbox{soft}{\parbox{0.965\linewidth}{
\textbf{Answer.} The four Pareto rows with $10^{-3}<\nrmse<0.05$ are not
genuine recoveries of the physical ground-truth equation.  All four omit the
variable input $I_{\rm ext}$; three also omit the standalone leak term $-0.3V$.
They pass only because the current SRBench-style checker rounds their
target-scaled coefficients and then accepts a constant symbolic difference.
}}

The ``rounded error'' column is the symbolic difference returned by the project
checker.  Each row is accepted by its \emph{difference-is-constant} rule; none
has zero unrounded difference and none has the full ground-truth support.

\section*{Concrete example: \texttt{d\_type}, seed 10002}

The ground-truth physical equation is
\begin{align*}
f_{\rm GT}={}&I_{\rm ext}
-120V\phina-36V\phik-9V\phi_D-0.3V\\
&+6000\phina-2772\phik-693\phi_D-16.32.
\end{align*}

The complexity-27 Pareto equation that the checker labels a match has
$\nrmse=0.003288$.  After undoing target scaling and expanding it, the equation is
approximately
\begin{align*}
\hat f={}&-120.196V\phina-36.163V\phik-9.168V\phi_D\\
&+5997.642\phina-2775.432\phik-695.790\phi_D-21.897.
\end{align*}

Most importantly, $\hat f$ has no $I_{\rm ext}$ term and no standalone $V$
term.  Its physical difference from the target is approximately
\begin{align*}
\hat f-f_{\rm GT}={}&-I_{\rm ext}+0.3V
-0.196V\phina-0.163V\phik-0.168V\phi_D\\
&-2.358\phina-3.432\phik-2.790\phi_D-5.577.
\end{align*}
This is plainly not an algebraic equivalent of the governing equation.

\section*{Why the checker accepts it}

PySR fits the target divided by its training RMS, which is roughly $5{,}400$ for
this world.  In the normalized equation, the exact coefficients become roughly
\[
I_{\rm ext}:\ 1.84\times10^{-4},
\qquad
V:\ -5.53\times10^{-5}.
\]
The project checker applies its SRBench-inspired floating-point cleanup before
symbolic simplification:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item constants smaller than $10^{-4}$ are replaced by zero;
  \item other floating constants are rounded to three decimal places; and
  \item a constant difference is accepted as a symbolic match.
\end{enumerate}

Consequently, the normalized $V$ coefficient is explicitly zeroed and the
$I_{\rm ext}$ coefficient rounds to $0.000$.  Other coefficient discrepancies
also vanish at this resolution.  What remains is the constant $0.001$, and the
constant-difference rule declares a match.

\section*{Conclusion}

\textcolor{warning}{\textbf{These four rows should not count as NeuronBench
ground-truth recoveries.}}  The issue is not merely that fitted constants differ
in their final decimals: the expressions omit variable-dependent terms from the
physical law.  The held-out NRMSE correctly exposes the omission, whereas the
combination of target scaling, coarse coefficient rounding, and the
constant-difference rule produces a symbolic false positive.

For NeuronBench, symbolic checks should operate on canonical expanded equations
in physical units, or use scale-aware coefficient tolerances that never erase a
ground-truth variable term.  Numerical recovery and polynomial-support equality
should remain separately reported.
